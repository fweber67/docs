FinanzDash is a platform for tax advisory and auditing firms that centrally visualizes and analyzes their clients' financial data and delivers it as interactive dashboards and AI-generated reports.

## What FinanzDash offers

- **Dashboards** – Interactive, customizable dashboards with KPI tiles, P&L reports, bank balance widgets, working capital charts, trend reports, and year comparisons
- **Company management** – Create, edit, and assign client companies to users
- **User management** – Invite users, assign roles, and control access
- **Data sources** – Upload financial data as a file (DATEV, BMD, other data sources) or retrieve it automatically via the DATEV interface
- **AI reports** – Create and publish financial reports with support from AI language models
- **DATEV integration** – OAuth connection to the DATEV API to retrieve trial balances automatically

## Who is FinanzDash for?

FinanzDash is used by two user groups:

1. **Firm employees** (administrators) – manage companies, users, data, and reports
2. **Client users** – have read-only access to the dashboards their firm has set up for them
