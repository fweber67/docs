FinanzDash uses email and password authentication. This page explains all steps from your first login to resetting your password.

## Signing in

1. Open FinanzDash in your browser
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click "Sign in"

After successful login, you will see the dashboard view or admin interface depending on your role.

### Account lockout

After multiple failed login attempts, your account will be temporarily locked. You will see a countdown showing how long you need to wait before you can try again.

## Set up password (Initial login)

When an administrator invites you to FinanzDash, you will receive a welcome email with a link to set up your password.

1. Open the email and click the link
2. You will be taken to a page where you can create a new password
3. Your password must be at least 12 characters long
4. After saving, you can sign in with your email and password

## Reset password

If you have forgotten your password:

1. Click "Forgot password?" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. You will receive an email with a link to reset your password
4. Click the link and create a new password

## Change language

FinanzDash is available in German and English. You can change the language using the language switcher in the top right of the sidebar. The setting is saved for your user account.

## Session monitoring

FinanzDash monitors your session for inactivity. If you are inactive for too long, you will be warned and can extend the session. With continued inactivity, you will be automatically logged out.
