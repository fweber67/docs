Automate recurring client reports and keep dashboards and summaries up to date.

**Problem**<br />For each client, manually compiling reports takes time that law firms and advisors would rather spend elsewhere. Reporting is often built ad hoc in Excel, making it hard to keep dashboards and summaries current and to compare results across clients.
